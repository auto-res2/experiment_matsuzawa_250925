# Experimental Design
- Strategy: Experiment 1 – Energy/Accuracy Pareto on Static Graphs
• Goal: Quantitatively prove that ECO-LASQ-GAT reaches the same accuracy as the best non-energy-aware optimiser while drawing fewer joules.
• Setup
  – Datasets: Reddit (medium) and OGBN-products (large, still fits 40 GB).
  – Hardware: 1 × A100-80 GB for all runs; 3 seeds.
  – Models: 6-layer and 12-layer GAT.
  – Methods: ECO-LASQ-GAT with λ_E∈{0,1e-4,5e-4,1e-3} and optional carbon_budget=65 kJ/epoch; baselines FP32, GACT-8b, AISQ-GAT, LASQ-GAT.
  – Measurements per epoch: test accuracy, wall-clock, nvml-reported joule, peak VRAM.
• Evaluation
  1. Plot Accuracy vs. Cumulative Joule; draw the Pareto frontier.
  2. Report “time-to-88 % accuracy per kWh”.
  3. Verify the 65 kJ budget: budgeted ECO-LASQ must stay < 65 kJ every epoch.
• Expected Outcome
  – Curves show ECO-LASQ dominating LASQ and all baselines: ≥ 99.7 % of LASQ accuracy at ≈ 65 % of the energy, confirming tri-objective knapsack effectiveness.
  – Budgeted run finishes in 1.1–1.2 × epochs yet never breaches the per-epoch cap, demonstrating on-line λ_E adaptation.
  – Memory footprint identical to LASQ (< 32 GB).
  – Proves that real-time power feedback yields a strictly better energy/accuracy trade-off.

Experiment 2 – Single-GPU Micro-Partitioning vs. Multi-GPU Data Parallelism
• Goal: Show that micro-partition off-loading lets a graph that normally overflows one GPU train efficiently on a single device, saving cluster energy.
• Setup
  – Dataset: OGBN-papers100M slice enlarged to 110 M edges so it no longer fits 40 GB.
  – Hardware:
     • ECO-LASQ: 1 × A100-80 GB with micro-partitioning.
     • Baseline: LASQ-GAT with PyTorch DDP on 4 × A100-80 GB (common fallback).
  – Equal global batch size; 3 seeds.
  – Metrics: accuracy, wall-clock, total joule across all GPUs, VRAM per GPU, inter-GPU traffic.
• Evaluation
  – Compare energy/epoch and time/epoch.
  – Compute “joule-per-correct-prediction” to factor accuracy.
• Expected Outcome
  – ECO-LASQ fits into 1 GPU (< 80 GB) while baseline needs 4 GPUs (≈ 60 GB each).
  – ECO-LASQ uses ≈ 35 % of the total energy and 40 % of the wall-clock of the 4-GPU run (because no NCCL overhead) at < 0.5 % accuracy loss.
  – Demonstrates that micro-partitioning is a viable alternative to costly data-parallel scaling, directly addressing Open Problem 3.

Experiment 3 – Dynamic Streaming Graph & Stability Gate Ablation
• Goal: Validate that the low-rank stability gate prevents catastrophic bit-allocation drift and maintains energy savings under distribution shift.
• Setup
  – Data: 1-hour KDD-Cup2021 streaming recommendations (edges appended every minute); replayed 10 × speed → 6-minute experiment.
  – Hardware: 2 × A100-80 GB (one processes current slice, one pre-fetches next) to isolate compute from I/O.
  – Methods: (a) ECO-LASQ (full), (b) ECO-LASQ without stability gate, (c) LASQ-GAT.
  – Metrics per time-window: accuracy@20, joule, λ_E value, Frobenius norm shift, bit-allocation variance σ_bits.
• Evaluation
  – Track σ_bits and accuracy over time; mark moments when ||A_t−Ā||_F>τ.
  – Compute energy saved vs. LASQ after shift events.
• Expected Outcome
  – With stability gate: σ_bits < 3 %, accuracy dip < 0.6 % and energy saving maintained at ≈ 34 %.
  – Without gate: σ_bits spikes to 18 %, accuracy drops > 4 % and λ_E oscillates, showing extra joule wastage.
  – Confirms that the gate delivers robustness promised in Open Problem 4 while preserving green benefits.

Collectively these three experiments cover: (1) the core energy/accuracy advantage, (2) memory-to-energy gains from micro-partitioning, and (3) stability under non-stationary workloads—demonstrating the full effectiveness of ECO-LASQ-GAT from multiple angles.

EXPERIMENT 1  –  ENERGY / ACCURACY PARETO ON STATIC GRAPHS
=================================================================
Goal
• Verify that ECO-LASQ-GAT attains (≈) the accuracy of the best non–energy-aware optimiser while drawing fewer joules and respecting an explicit 65 kJ · epoch⁻¹ carbon budget.

1. Models
   – Target: ECO-LASQ-GAT (6-layer, 12-layer)
   – Baselines (identical layer widths & heads)
     • Plain GAT FP-32 (DGL implementation)
     • GACT-8b (Chen et al., 2022)
     • AISQ-GAT (Yuan et al., 2023)
     • LASQ-GAT (our ablation λ_E = 0)
     • Degree-Quant INT-4 (Hu et al., 2021)

2. Datasets
   – Reddit (230 K nodes / 11 M edges)
   – OGBN-products (2.4 M nodes / 123 M edges)

3. Dataset preprocessing
   • Standardise node features to μ = 0, σ = 1 (fit on training nodes only).
   • Drop isolated nodes.  (< 0.04 %)
   • Cast features → FP16 for all low-precision methods to avoid mixed-type copies.

4. Data split
   • Predefined OGB splits: train / valid / test (cf. OGB leader-board).
   • Reddit: 70 / 15 / 15 random split stratified by label; same three seeds (13, 17, 23).

5. Training protocol
   • Optimiser: AdamW, lr = 2.5 × 10⁻³, β = (0.9, 0.999), weight-decay = 5 × 10⁻⁴.
   • Scheduler: cosine decay w/ linear warm-up 5 epochs.
   • Batch size: full-graph, mini-batch neighbour-sampling disabled ⇒ deterministic FLOPs between methods.
   • Epochs: 300 (Reddit) / 200 (Products) or early-stop (patience = 40 on valid accuracy).

6. Hyper-parameters studied
   • λ_E ∈ {0, 1e-4, 5e-4, 1e-3}
   • carbon_budget ∈ {None, 65 kJ}
   • λ_M fixed to 1 × 10⁻⁴ (best from LASQ paper).
   • k_l (target sparsity) initialised to 0.5·d_out, bits ∈ {2,4,6,8} – updated by tri-objective rule.

7. Repetitions & selection criteria
   • 3 seeds.  Report mean ± s.d.
   • Best-val checkpoint exported (max validation accuracy); “last” and “early-stop” logged for reproducibility.
   • Primary reporting = best-val → test.

8. Evaluation metrics
   Primary
   • Test accuracy [%]
   • Cumulative joules [J] (NVML-integrated ∑ power·Δt)
   Secondary
   • Wall-clock per epoch [s]
   • Peak VRAM [GB] (torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated)
   • “time-to-88 % accuracy per kWh” [min / kWh]
   • CO₂eq [g] (joule × regional factor = 475 g · kWh⁻¹)
   • FLOPs / epoch (ptflops, on forward pass only†)

9. Robustness checks
   • Add 5 % Gaussian noise to node features; report Δ test-acc.
   • OOD split: hold out electronics category in OGBN-products; evaluate when unseen at train time.

10. Hyper-parameter importance diagnostics
   • 5-point logarithmic sweep of λ_E with fixed others; fit quadratic surrogate ⇒ ANOVA F-score.
   • Bits vs. accuracy heat-map aggregated over layers.

11. Resource accounting
   • Automatic torch-profiler hooks collect CUDA time, CPU time, peak host-RAM.
   • Cost ≈ AWS p4d.24xlarge spot (8×A100) ⇒ 11.57 USD · h⁻¹; cost/experiment computed.

Goal
Demonstrate that micro-partitioning lets a 110 M-edge graph train on one A100, outperforming 4-GPU DDP in energy and wall-clock.

   • Forward FLOPs identical; communication FLOPs counted separately for DDP.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Goal
Validate robustness of the low-rank stability gate under distribution shift using the KDD-Cup-2021 streaming recommendation dump.

1. Dataset
   • We replay the 1-h stream at 10× speed (≈ 360 batches, 12 s each).
   • The dump contains user, item, category, timestamp ⇒ converted to bipartite graph every minute via sliding-window (30 min history).
   • Node features: TF-IDF on textual meta-data + one-hot day-of-week.

2. Models / Variants
   a) ECO-LASQ-GAT (full) – stability_gate ON
   b) ECO-LASQ-GAT – stability_gate OFF
   c) LASQ-GAT (λ_E = 0)
   All 6-layer, hidden = 256.

3. Hardware
   • GPU-0: training; GPU-1: asynchronous data pre-processing (CUDA IPC pipe).

4. Training procedure
   • Online mini-batch (128 k interactions / step);
   • Learning-rate η_t ∝ 1 / √t to ensure stability.
   • λ_E starts at 0, warm-up to 5 e-4 by step 100.

5. Metrics gathered every slice (12 s real-time)
   – accuracy@20 (hit ratio) on last 2 k interactions
   – joule / slice (sum both GPUs)
   – λ_E value, σ_bits (std across 6 layers), ‖A_t – Ā‖_F

6. Robustness & drift events
   • Shift flagged when ‖A_t – Ā‖_F > τ = 0.15·‖Ā‖_F.
   • We inject label noise on 3 random minutes by permuting 20 % targets; analyse recovery time.

7. Termination
   • 360 steps; early stop disabled.

8. Resource & cost logging identical to Exp 1; communication between GPUs measured via NVLink counters.
