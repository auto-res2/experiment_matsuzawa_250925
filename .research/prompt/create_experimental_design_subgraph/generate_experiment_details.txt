# Experiment Strategy
Experiment 1: Cross-domain Few-shot Benchmark (Domain-conditioned Spectral Basis)
Goal: Show that AdaSProNet’s hyper-network H_ϕ can bend the spectral sub-space to each domain, outperforming single-basis methods when source and target differ strongly.
Setup
• Datasets: mini-ImageNet → test on natural images (Meta-Dataset/Imagenet), AND train on mini-ImageNet but test on dermatology PAD-UFES-20 (medical) and Birds (fine-grained). 500 episodes each.
• 5-way 1-shot & 5-shot.
• Models: AdaSProNet (full), Spectral-ProtoNet (global basis), ProtoNet, FLUTE.
• Hardware: single A100 (batching 16 episodes). Runs <48 h.
Metrics
• Mean episode accuracy; paired t-test at 95 % CI.
• Expected Calibration Error (ECE).
• Active rank R_t distribution per domain.
• Inference latency per query.
Expected Result
• AdaSProNet wins +2 – 3 % acc. on natural, +4 – 6 % on medical; ECE halves vs. Spectral-ProtoNet.
• Active rank adapts: avg 7 for natural, 3 for medical, 10 for fine-grained; latency scales accordingly (<1.3× ProtoNet).
Demonstrates
Adaptive, domain-conditioned basis yields higher accuracy and better calibration without global overhead.

Experiment 2: Rank-Sparsity & Capacity Efficiency Stress-Test
Goal: Quantify how Gumbel-gated rank selection lets the network match task intrinsic complexity, avoiding over-/under-fitting and saving FLOPs.
Setup
• Synthetic controllable dataset: Omniglot characters (low intrinsic dimension), CUB-200 fine-grained birds (high).
• Generate “mixed” meta-episodes where intrinsic rank is controlled by adding Gaussian directions to embeddings before support/query split.
• Compare: (a) AdaSProNet, (b) AdaSProNet w/ rank fixed to median 8, (c) Spectral-ProtoNet (global rank 10), (d) ProtoNet.
• Measure: accuracy vs. injected intrinsic rank (1…15), average active rank, GFLOPs/query.
Expected Result
• Accuracy of AdaSProNet tracks oracle upper bound within <1 %; fixed-rank degrades outside sweet spot (±4 %).
• FLOPs scale linearly with active rank; up to 40 % saving on low-rank tasks.
• Gumbel gates select correct rank ±1 in 85 % episodes.
Demonstrates
Automatic rank-sparsity matches capacity to task difficulty, maintaining performance while reducing computation.

Experiment 3: Continual Few-shot Stream with Safety Monitoring
Goal: Validate continual sub-space update and uncertainty-aware rejection in a deployment-like stream.
Setup
• 20 sequential domains from Meta-Dataset (ordered: Aircraft→Flowers→Signs→Dermatology, etc.). 5-way 5-shot per domain, 1,000 episodes/domain in arrival order. FIFO buffer size 1 k.
• Models: AdaSProNet (with/without online update), CWR-ProtoNet, FLUTE.
• After each new domain, replay 200 held-out episodes from all previous domains to measure forgetting and calibration drift.
Metrics
• Accuracy current domain; forgetting Δacc previous domains.
• ECE drift over time.
• Coverage vs. risk when rejecting predictions with confidence <τ (τ∈{0.1…0.5}).
• GPU memory & update time.
Expected Result
• AdaSProNet w/ updates retains ≥97 % of original accuracy on earlier domains (forgetting ≤1 %); no-update variant drops 6 %. Baselines ≥7 %.
• ECE remains ≤5 % for updated AdaSProNet, drifts to 12 % without updates.
• At τ=0.3, critical false positives drop 2× with only 8 % coverage loss.
• Update step <0.5 s on A100, <500 MB RAM.
Demonstrates
Lightweight continual adaptation preserves knowledge, sustains calibration, and provides actionable uncertainty estimates suitable for safety-critical continual few-shot deployment.
