The study employs a theoretical approach by deriving a conservation law for GATs with positive homogeneous activation functions (e.g., ReLUs), which explains the relationship between model parameters and their gradients under gradient flow. This conservation law reveals that unbalanced initial norms of feature and attention weights hinder gradient propagation. Based on this insight, the authors propose a parameter balancing procedure: 1) initializing all attention parameters to zero, 2) scaling the initial squared norms of incoming feature weights to the first layer, and 3) recursively scaling outgoing feature weights to ensure a balanced state where a specific norm equality holds across layers. Two specific balanced initialization schemes, Balanced Xavier (BalX) and Balanced Orthogonal (BalO) with a looks-linear mirrored block structure, are developed and evaluated.
