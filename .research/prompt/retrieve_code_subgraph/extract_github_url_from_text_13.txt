# Paper Outline
The core methodology involves deriving a conservation law for GATs with positive homogeneous activation functions (e.g., ReLU, LeakyReLU). This law reveals a structural relationship between model parameters and their gradients, specifically that the difference between the squared L2-norms of incoming and outgoing parameters to a neuron, adjusted for attention parameters, remains approximately constant under gradient flow. Based on this insight, the paper proposes a 'balanced initialization' procedure. This scheme involves setting attention parameters to zero and scaling feature transformation weights (Wl) such that the norm preservation condition (Wl[i, :]^2 - al[i]^2 = Wl+1[: i]^2) is met layer-wise. An advanced variant, Balanced Orthogonal Initialization (BalO), incorporates a 'looks-linear mirrored block structure' orthogonal initialization for feature weights, aiming to enhance dynamical isometry before applying the balancing procedure.
