The core methodology involves reformulating the sampling variance optimization problem as an adversary bandit problem. Rewards are defined as the negative derivatives of the effective variance, which are related to node embeddings and learned weights that change during training. Two bandit algorithms are proposed: 1. GNN-BS, based on the Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB) setting, where a single neighbor is sampled `k` times, and the sampler is updated by EXP3. 2. GNN-BS.M, based on MAB with multiple plays, which uses an efficient `k`-combination sampler (DepRound) to sample a `k`-element subset of neighbors once, updating the sampler with EXP3.M.
