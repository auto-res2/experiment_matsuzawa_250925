TENT optimizes the model during testing by minimizing the Shannon entropy H(ˆy) of its predictions ˆy= fθ(xt). This is an unsupervised objective that relies on the model's confidence. Instead of optimizing the full model parameters θ, TENT focuses on modulating features through low-dimensional, channel-wise affine transformations. Specifically, it repurposes the normalization layers of the source model, estimating their normalization statistics (mean µ and standard deviation σ) online from each target batch during the forward pass and optimizing their affine parameters (scale γ and shift β) via gradient descent on the prediction entropy during the backward pass. This constrained parameterization (<1% of total model parameters) ensures efficiency and stability for online test-time adaptation, requiring just one gradient per test point.
