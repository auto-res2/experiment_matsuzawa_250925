The core methodology involves deriving a conservation law for GAT gradient flow dynamics. This derivation is based on the concept of rescale invariance, adapted from traditional deep neural networks, and applies to GATs with positive homogeneous activation functions (e.g., ReLU, LeakyReLU). The conservation law reveals a structural relationship between parameters and their gradients. Based on this theoretical insight, a balanced initialization scheme is devised. This scheme involves setting attention parameters to zero and scaling feature transformation weights layer-wise to ensure that the l2-norms of incoming and outgoing weights satisfy a specific balancedness condition (∥Wl[i, :]∥² - ∥al[i]∥² = ∥Wl+1[:, i]∥²).
